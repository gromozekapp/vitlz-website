1. Delivery area

We ship to all countries within the European Union. If your country is not available at checkout, delivery to your location is not currently offered.

2. Order processing

Orders are processed on business days (Monday–Friday, excluding Bulgarian public holidays). Processing time: 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. You will receive email confirmation when your order is placed and when it ships.

We ship via Econt, Speedy, or Interlogistics, depending on destination, package size, and service availability.

Estimated delivery after dispatch: 1–6 business days within the EU. Times are estimates only, not guaranteed.

Shipping is calculated at checkout. Standard EU shipping typically ranges from €2.00 to €8.50. The exact amount is shown before you complete your purchase.

When available, a tracking number is sent by email after dispatch.
