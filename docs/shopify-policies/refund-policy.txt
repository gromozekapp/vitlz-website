1. Overview
Unless mandatory consumer law provides otherwise, you may request a return within 30 days of delivery, provided the conditions below are met.

For customers in the European Union, the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal also applies as described in Section 6.

2. How to request a return
Contact us at info@vitlz.eu before sending anything back. Include your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason for the return.

We will review your request and, if approved, provide return instructions and a return authorization (RMA) number. Returns sent without prior approval or without an RMA number may be refused or delayed.

3. Conditions for acceptance
To be eligible for a return (other than a defective or incorrect item), the product must be:
• unused and in the same condition as received;
• in its original, unopened, factory-sealed packaging (blister, safety seal, and outer box intact);
• complete, with all items, leaflets, and free gifts included;
• accompanied by proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt).

We do not accept returns of food supplements, probiotics, vitamins, or similar products once the protective or security packaging has been opened, broken, or tampered with after delivery.

4. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items
Please inspect your order upon delivery. If you receive a defective, damaged, or wrong item, contact us at info@vitlz.eu within 48 hours of delivery, including your order number and clear photos of:
• the outer shipping box;
• the product packaging;
• the defect or damage.

We will assess the case and propose an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable law (repair, replacement, or refund where required).

5. Non-returnable items
The following cannot be returned, except where required by law or where the product is defective or not as described:
• opened or unsealed food supplements, probiotics, vitamin complexes, and similar health products (see Section 6);
• items that are not in resaleable condition due to handling beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics, and functioning;
• gift cards;
• items marked as final sale, if clearly stated at checkout.

6. European Union — 14-day right of withdrawal
If you are a consumer in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason.

To exercise this right, notify us clearly (e.g. by email to info@vitlz.eu) within the 14-day period.

Health and hygiene exception (Article 16(e) of Directive 2011/83/EU):
The right of withdrawal does not apply to sealed goods which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons if unsealed after delivery. This includes all food supplements, probiotics, and vitamin complexes in our range once the protective packaging has been opened.

If withdrawal is valid and the product is returned unopened and in resaleable condition:
• you are responsible for the direct cost of returning the goods, unless we state otherwise or applicable law requires otherwise;
• we may deduct an amount for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics, and functioning;
• unless we have offered to collect the goods ourselves, we may withhold reimbursement until we have received the goods back or until you have supplied evidence of having sent them back, whichever is earlier.

Original delivery charges are non-refundable except where the goods are defective, not as described, or where applicable law requires a refund of delivery costs.

7. Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. If you wish to exchange a product, please request a return (if eligible) and place a new order separately.

8. Refunds
After we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you whether your refund is approved.

If approved, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval. Your bank or card provider may need additional time to post the credit.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your refund, contact us at info@vitlz.eu.

9. Refusal of returns
We may refuse a return or partial refund if:
• the return was not pre-approved;
• the product does not meet the conditions in this policy;
• the product shows signs of use, missing items, or opened hygiene packaging;
• the statutory withdrawal period has expired and no legal remedy applies.

10. Questions
For any questions about returns or refunds: info@vitlz.eu
