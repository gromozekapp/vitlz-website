1. Overview

Unless mandatory consumer law provides otherwise, you may request a return within 30 days of delivery, provided the conditions below are met. For customers in the European Union, the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal also applies as described in Section 6.

2. How to request a return

Contact us at info@vitlz.eu before sending anything back. Include your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason. If approved, we provide return instructions and an RMA number. Returns without prior approval may be refused or delayed.

3. Conditions for acceptance

To be eligible (other than defective or incorrect items), the product must be unused, in original factory-sealed packaging (blister and safety seal intact), complete with all leaflets and gifts, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

We do not accept returns of food supplements, probiotics, or vitamins once protective packaging has been opened, broken, or tampered with after delivery.

4. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

Inspect your order upon delivery. Contact us within 48 hours at info@vitlz.eu with your order number and photos of the shipping box, product packaging, and defect. We will propose an appropriate remedy under applicable law.

5. Non-returnable items

  • Opened or unsealed supplements and vitamin products (see Section 6)
  • Items not in resaleable condition due to excessive handling
  • Gift cards and final-sale items where stated at checkout

6. EU — 14-day right of withdrawal

EU consumers may withdraw within 14 days of receiving goods without giving a reason by notifying us at info@vitlz.eu.

Health and hygiene exception (Article 16(e), Directive 2011/83/EU): Withdrawal does not apply to sealed goods unsuitable for return for health or hygiene reasons if unsealed after delivery. This includes all food supplements, probiotics, and vitamin complexes once packaging is opened.

If withdrawal is valid and goods are returned unopened: you bear return shipping costs unless we state otherwise; we may deduct value lost through handling beyond normal inspection; we may withhold refund until goods are received or proof of return is provided. Original delivery charges are non-refundable except where required by law.

7. Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Request a return (if eligible) and place a new order separately.

8. Refunds

After inspection, we notify you if your refund is approved. Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 10 business days. Banks may need additional processing time. Contact us if more than 15 business days pass after approval.

9. Refusal of returns

We may refuse returns that were not pre-approved, do not meet this policy, show use or opened hygiene packaging, or fall outside statutory periods without a legal remedy.
